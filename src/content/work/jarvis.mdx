Jarvis is the daily-driver AI assistant I built for myself. It runs as a launchd
daemon on macOS, listens for a global hotkey, streams audio through whisper.cpp,
and hands the transcript to Claude via MCP tools.

The design principle: every tool is deterministic and reversible. The model is
the only source of ambiguity in the loop.
