title: Why agents need tools
slug: why-agents-need-tools
date: 2026-06-11
dek: An agent without a tool surface is just a chatbot with better prose.
readingTime: 6 min read
---

An agent is only as useful as the surface it can touch. Give a model access to a
browser, a shell, an email inbox, and a filesystem — you get a colleague. Give
it none of those — you get a smart intern with their hands tied.
